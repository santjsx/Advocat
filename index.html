Privacy Policy for Advocat

Last Updated: March 9, 2026

Advocat ("we," "our," or "us") operates the Advocat mobile application (the "Service"). We take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy outlines what information we collect, how we use it, and how we protect it when you use our legal practice management system.

1. Information Collection and the Offline-First Approach

Advocat is designed as an offline-first application. This means that we do not collect, transmit, or store your personal data, your clients' case data, sensitive documents, or legal notes on our servers.

All case metadata, client information, documents, and schedules you create within the app are strictly stored and encrypted locally on your personal device using secure storage mechanisms.

2. Usage Data

Since Advocat operates primarily without an internet connection, we do not inherently collect diagnostic telemetry or track user behavior.

If the app experiences a crash or error, your device's operating system (Android or iOS) may independently gather generic error reports according to your device settings (e.g., through Google Play Console crash reporting). These anonymous crash logs contain no personally identifiable legal or case information and are only meant to help us diagnose app stability.

3. Data Storage and Security

We utilize local device storage encryption (such as AsyncStorage and local filesystem APIs) to ensure your data remains secure on your device. We do not maintain any cloud databases or possess the keys to decrypt or view your local case information.

Because your data is exclusively stored on your device, you are solely responsible for backing up your data and securing access to your physical unit.

4. Notifications

Advocat utilizes local notification services to remind you of critical deadlines and court schedules. These notifications are scheduled and processed entirely on your device. No schedule data is transmitted over the internet to trigger these alerts.

5. Information Sharing and Third Parties

Because we do not collect your personal data or case files, we cannot and do not sell, rent, or share your information with any third parties.

6. Children's Privacy

Our Service is intended for legal professionals and individuals aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy within the app or updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this document.
